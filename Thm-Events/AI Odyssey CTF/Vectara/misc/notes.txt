#observation

The log is loudly shouting supply chain compromise / insecure deployment pipeline.

The most suspicious line is this:

CHECK template_source: external-registry-7.tryhaulme.net(expected: internal.tryhaulme-registry.net)[WARNING: unapproved registry]

That means the system expected templates/configs from a trusted internal registry, but instead pulled something from an external source.

Then this gets even worse:

[integrity_check: bypassed via template directive]

That suggests the deployment template itself contained instructions that disabled or bypassed integrity verification.

So the likely attack chain is:

A malicious or unauthorized template was introduced from:

external-registry-7.tryhaulme.net
The template included a directive to bypass integrity checks.

A deployment occurred:

sha256:b3f1c9a4d7e20f81a5c2b8e4f0d9c3a7

The audit tool detected the anomaly and exited with:

exit 1

Key indicators of compromise:

Unapproved external registry
Integrity verification bypass
Template-based policy manipulation
Deployment immediately after suspicious config load
